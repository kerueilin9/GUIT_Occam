type [id] [content] [press_enter_after=0|1]: To type content into a field with a specific ID. By default, the "Enter" key is pressed after typing unless `press_enter_after` is set to 0. For any field where typing is likely to open a date picker or calendar popup, always use `press_enter_after=1` to confirm the date and close the popup. Use `press_enter_after=0` when pressing Enter may submit the form, finish the task early, or otherwise trigger an unwanted action. E.g., `type [15] [Carnegie Mellon University] [1]`; for a Finish date field, use `type [Finish_id] [5/19/26] [1]`.
