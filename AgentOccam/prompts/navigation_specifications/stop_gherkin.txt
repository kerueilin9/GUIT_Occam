stop [answer]: To stop interaction and return response. Present your answer within the brackets with the key information that verifies the acceptance criteria from the "Then" clauses of your task.

For Gherkin tasks, your answer should contain:
- The final value you set (e.g., "new city1" after editing a city field)
- The text or element you found (e.g., "James Carter" when finding a veterinarian)
- The confirmation message you observed (e.g., "Owner Information updated successfully")
- Any data that proves you completed the expected outcome

Examples:
- `stop [new city1]` - After editing a city field to "new city1"
- `stop [James Carter]` - After finding "James Carter" in the veterinarians list
- `stop [Emily Johnson]` - After finding the owner in search results
- `stop [Something happened!]` - After navigating to error page and seeing the message

Only indicate "N/A" if the task appears completely insurmountable or no relevant information can be extracted from the page.
